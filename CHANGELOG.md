# Changelog

## 0.1.0 · first release

- **Demo mode**: five simulated agents, a guided tour and a sound lesson that teaches every signal.
- **Live mode**: your running Claude Code sessions play, read-only. Every musician stands for a kind of work. Codex CLI and Gemini CLI sessions play too.
- **The fermata**: when Claude Code asks for permission, the hall holds a chord until you press Approve or Deny in Bytehoven.
- **Conducting**: hand a musician a new part (`claude -p`, narrow permissions), or let the conductor plan first and show you the plan.
- **Themes**: Orchestra, Jazz club, Lo-fi room and Chiptune arcade.
- **Mixer**, **Box office** (today's tokens per musician), and **Today's symphony** (record a short video).
- **`npx bytehoven`**: one command, no dependencies, on 127.0.0.1 only; live mode needs the key from your terminal.
